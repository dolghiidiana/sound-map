# Sound Map for a Writer's Scene

Descriptive working label; the project name is undecided.

A writer describes one scene, hears an AI-proposed sound map, and reshapes it into a saved sonic blueprint to support writing decisions.

## The Unique Kernel

AI as a creative perception tool: the writer can hear how changing sonic relationships changes their interpretation of a scene. Explicitly described sounds remain distinct from AI-suggested sounds, making the interpretation open to disagreement and revision.

## Who It's For

One writer with a scene in mind or a short description, seeking to explore its atmosphere more concretely. They want to hear the world well enough to make creative decisions, rather than produce professional sound design. Their existing tools or methods have not been established.

## The Core Loop

Describe one scene -> AI interprets it using available sounds -> hear and see the proposed map -> move or adjust sounds -> hear the difference -> save the resulting map/blueprint as a creative reference while writing.

## Inspiration & Identity

Exploration, imagination, and perception with a tangible outcome. The central interaction is a movable sound map with essential audible feedback. Visual style remains open for the product-requirements conversation.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

Diana wants to learn to turn a creative AI idea into a small interactive product while participating in decisions about AI versus ordinary software, audio behavior, feasibility, and testing the experience with another person.

## What "Working" Looks Like

A writer can complete the core loop for one supported scene, hear the effect of their changes, and save a blueprint capturing their choices.

Diana's example: a nearly empty cafe near closing, rain outside, staff cleaning in the background. The initial cleaning sounds feel too close. Moving them farther away and emphasizing the rain helps the writer decide that the character feels more isolated from the room. This is an illustrative creative outcome, not an emotion the system must prescribe or guarantee.

Proposed evidence of value: another person can make an intentional adjustment and explain what it helped them decide about the scene. This has not been tested.

## The POC Boundary

- One writer, one scene, one short sound map.
- A small licensed library with three to five layers per scene and a rough audible preview.
- AI proposes an interpretation; the writer owns the resulting choices.
- Explicit and suggested sounds are distinguishable.
- A movable map changes audible relationships. Candidate controls include level, left/right position, approximate distance, presence, and silence; their exact meanings and feasible first set will be resolved in PRD/spec.
- A saved sound map/sonic blueprint is the required creative result; its format remains undecided.
- A focused end-to-end demonstration and public repository with planning documents are the submission target. Deployment is optional. Aim for the challenge's small 2-4-hour active-work experiment; feasibility is unproven and must guide cuts.

## Later

- Other creative audiences beyond writers.
- Polished generative audio and broader sound coverage.
- Finished audio-production exports, if later demonstrated useful; not required to prove the current outcome.

## Explicitly Cut

- A professional sound-design editor: exceeds the writer's immediate purpose.
- Supporting every imaginable scene: a small library needs an honest supported range.
- Claiming to reconstruct a memory or determine its true emotional meaning: the system offers a revisable interpretation.

## Risks to Resolve

- Value: enjoyable manipulation might not lead to a useful writing decision.
- Technical: available recordings and approximate spatial controls might not convey the intended relationships.
- Unsupported sounds need an honest treatment rather than silent substitution; the exact behavior belongs in PRD.

Scope approved by Diana on September 24, 2026. No implementation has begun. Product behavior and technical feasibility remain to be resolved in PRD and spec; prototype evidence has not yet been collected.
